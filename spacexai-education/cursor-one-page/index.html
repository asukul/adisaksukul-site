If it works, the design that scales

A named educator applies per course; students redeem a capped coupon that expires at term end; tripwires on redemption speed and location claw credit back. It is the Google Cloud education-credit model, which stayed open where open student sign-ups did not. Details and the fraud comparison are on the full page, if useful.
