AI 2010 with a class bot: how the course runs for one semester on Grok Bot Team Bots

AI 2010 is Iowa State's introductory applied AI course for non-majors, the course that led to the university-wide Applied AI Minor. About 150 students in spring 2027, three lectures a week plus a weekly lab, no programming prerequisite. This outline shows where the three Team Bots appear, which assignments allow AI and at what level, and what is measured each week. Topics follow the existing syllabus; only the bot layer is new.

Students 150, mixed majors; 2 instructors and 4 TAsPlan Grok Bot on Teams, one course teamData synthetic systems only; no LMS connector, no student email, no submissions in the botTerm 14 teaching weeks, late January to early May

The three bots the instructor publishes

BotRoleRules, in plain words
TutorEvery student, all termHints and questions first; a worked example of a different problem; the final answer only after three attempts, then explained. Never a submission-ready artifact.
Lab partnerWeekly lab, in the roomWorks only on the sandbox: a sample CRM, a mailbox of synthetic tickets, a demo storefront. Explains every action before running it; stops for approval on anything that writes.
Monday routineOne shared routine, weeklyCompiles the week's reading questions and a short news digest for the discipline themes that week. Read-only; no connectors beyond the web.

AI level per assignment

Each assignment carries one of five levels from the AI Assessment Scale (Perkins, Roe and Furze). The level is not a sentence in the syllabus; it is which bot is open and how it is configured.

What ISU provides, what SpaceXAI provides

ISU: two instructors and four TAs, one named course-team admin, the course material and the three class bots, a comparison section taught without the bots, and the public write-up with a factual review by SpaceXAI before release. SpaceXAI: education credit for 156 seats for the semester, in whatever form is simplest, a named contact, and a 30-minute onboarding for the instructors. Nothing on this page requires a feature SpaceXAI does not ship today.
