TheAIProgram.org · AI for High School Teachers

A complete learning platform I built from scratch — live at TheAIProgram.org. Its flagship, Iowa's first statewide AI course for teachers, delivered its first cohort in July 2026 — and a Quantum ML & AI course (QAI 1010) is next.

  • Full LMS: enrollment, auto-graded exercises, a Canvas-exportable gradebook, discussions, and course certificates.
  • In-browser Python (Pyodide) with CodeLens line-by-line execution — no install, no setup, built for beginners.
  • A Socratic AI tutor on every step: a three-rung hint ladder (nudge → strategy → worked step) that deliberately withholds full solutions.
  • Anchored to the UNESCO AI Competency Framework — teachers from any subject, statewide, no coding required.

Project lead · LLM fine-tuning

PyTutor AI

I lead PyTutor AI — an open-weight LLM I fine-tuned for Socratic teaching, guiding questions first — supported by ISU's Office of the Provost and the Department of Computer Science.

  • Custom Socratic fine-tune of a 27B open-weight model for CS, DS, ML, and AI coursework — SFT + DPO with a pedagogical reward model that favors hint-first responses.
  • Five-benchmark evaluation stack that scores teaching quality, not just answer accuracy.
  • Trained and served on Nova, ISU's GPU research cluster.

Lead technical architect · since 2010

LIRT: Retrieval at National Scale

I designed and lead the technical architecture of the Legislative Institutional Repository of Thailand (LIRT), the country's largest institutional repository by document count: about 120,000 mostly Thai-language legislative documents.

  • RAG search over the full collection (OAI-PMH ingestion → vector index), so legislative records can be asked about in plain language.
  • OCR ingestion that turns scanned PDFs and archives into retrievable, citable knowledge.
  • Embedding and inference jobs scaled across GPU nodes on ISU's Nova cluster; Qdrant, pgvector and Gemini File Search evaluated head to head. Contributor to the NIST TREC RAG track.

Benchmark · NIST TREC 2026

Video Question Answering

Team iastate's entry in the TREC 2026 Video QA track tested how far off-the-shelf multimodal models go with no fine-tuning, when runs are chosen by calibration rather than by hindsight.

  • Vote and rank-fusion ensembles over off-the-shelf multimodal models; no fine-tuning.
  • Runs selected on 573 calibration questions rebuilt from the 2025 data, with expected results recorded before any 2026 results came back.
  • Our best multiple-choice run had the highest Top-1 accuracy of 42 submitted runs, within a statistically tied top group. Talk at TREC 2026, November 18.

Multi-model · LMS

Multi-Vendor AI Teaching Assistant

A course AI assistant that runs on several models at once — so students aren't locked to one vendor, and neither is the university.

  • Deploys open models (Llama 4 Scout, Phi-4, gpt-oss, Gemma, Nemotron) on Azure AI Foundry for ~100 students per course.
  • A Canvas-LTI RAG "living textbook" grounded in each course's own materials and owned by ISU.
  • Multi-vendor by design (Claude / GPT / Gemini) to avoid single-vendor lock-in.
